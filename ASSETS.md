# Asset Manifest Notes

All shipped creature, environment, cover, and app-icon artwork is project-original generated art or a derivative/packing of that generated art. No official My Singing Monsters art, audio, or proprietary game assets are included.

The 104 creature source renders are packed into two portrait atlases plus twelve articulated rig atlases (six part types across two pages). Packing preserves the under-100-file GitHub/iOS delivery rule without deleting roster art.

Audio files are original rendered WAV assets made for this build. They are not copied from proprietary game files or commercial sample packs.

The machine-readable inventory is `asset-manifest.json`.
